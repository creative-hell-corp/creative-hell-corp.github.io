Privacy notice

Effective date: 5 October 2026.

This notice explains how AutoVid handles information when its operator connects a Google account. AutoVid is a private tool for the operator’s own publishing workflow.

Information accessed and stored

AutoVid uses Google OAuth access and refresh tokens to call the YouTube Data API. It reads the connected channel’s ID and basic channel details. It sends video files, titles, descriptions, and visibility settings to YouTube. It stores upload status, video IDs, scheduling records, and operational error records.

Use and sharing

This information is used to identify the correct channel, upload and manage its videos, and prevent duplicate uploads. OAuth credentials and posting records are stored in private files on the operator’s computers and server. They are not published on this website.

Video files and metadata are sent to YouTube to perform uploads. AutoVid does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Its separate video preparation process can use AI services for scripts, images, and narration; Google OAuth credentials are not inputs to that process.

AutoVid’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are kept while the connection is in use. Posting records and video files remain until the operator deletes them. AutoVid does not currently apply an automatic retention period.

To disconnect Google, remove AutoVid from Google Account connections. The operator can then delete the saved Google credentials and local posting records, including retained backup copies. Removing access does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube. Those videos can be deleted in YouTube Studio.

This website

This static website does not include app sign-in, advertising, analytics scripts, or forms that collect personal information. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
