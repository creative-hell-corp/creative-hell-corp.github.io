AutoVid

AutoVid is a private tool that prepares short story videos and schedules uploads to accounts controlled by its operator. It supports the Historias Bajo Llave channels.

How AutoVid uses Google

The operator connects a Google account through Google OAuth. AutoVid uses the YouTube Data API to identify the connected channel, upload videos with titles and descriptions, check upload status, and manage video visibility when enabled.

AutoVid runs on the operator’s computers and server. Access is limited to the operator. This website provides information about the app; it does not offer account registration or Google sign-in.

Account control

The operator chooses the videos, connected accounts, schedule, and publication settings. Google access can be removed at any time through the Google Account permissions page.
